ZenIcon: additional third-party glyph sources
===========================================

These notices record source attribution and unresolved license information.
They are not a replacement for missing permission or a grant of new rights.

The glyph paths in lib/icons/assets/selection.json match the named source
sets in lib/icons/assets/zenicon.json. The selected glyphs are included in
the ZenIcon fonts under lib/icons/public/.
